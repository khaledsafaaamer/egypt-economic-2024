🕌 Cairo's markets reflect the inflation pressures faced by Egyptian households in 2024

Egypt's 2024 economic story is one of structural reform under pressure. The March 2024 currency devaluation — moving the EGP from ~30 to ~50 per USD — was painful but essential to unlock IMF disbursements and restore FX market confidence. Combined with a $35B UAE investment deal, the foundations for stabilization were laid, even as households endured the highest inflation in decades.

EGP Devaluation Timeline

The Egyptian Pound lost approximately 68% of its value between early 2022 and end-2024 — a painful but necessary adjustment.

Second devaluation. Dollar shortage intensifies. Parallel market premium widens.

Third devaluation. Inflation accelerates. IMF negotiations intensify.

Policy Actions

Key Reforms 2024

Egypt's reform program is multi-dimensional — combining monetary tightening, fiscal consolidation, structural reforms, and digital transformation.

1
Monetary Tightening
CBE raised benchmark rates to 27% — one of the world's highest. Aims to anchor inflation expectations and defend EGP.
2
SOE Privatization
35+ state-owned companies listed for sale. Targets $5-7B in proceeds. Military-affiliated firms partially included.
3
Subsidy Reform
Fuel and electricity subsidies cut by $2B/year. Expanded Takaful/Karama social programs offset impact on poor.
4
Exchange Rate Liberalization
March 2024 EGP float eliminated the parallel market premium (~70+ EGP/USD black market). Restored FX market confidence.
5
Digital Economy
Digital payments grew 15%. 100+ licensed fintechs. E-invoicing mandate. ~70% of gov't services now digital.
6
Tax Reform
Broadened tax base, improved collection efficiency. Total government revenue reached £1.5 Trillion in FY2023-24, +15% YoY.
