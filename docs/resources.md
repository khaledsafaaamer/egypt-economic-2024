## 📰 Key Publications to Read

<details>
<summary><strong>Essential Reports (Free to Download)</strong></summary>

1. **IMF Egypt: 2024 Article IV Consultation** — The most comprehensive external assessment of Egypt's economy. Reviews all macro indicators, risks, and reform progress. Available at IMF.org.

2. **World Bank Egypt Economic Monitor** — Published semi-annually; focus on fiscal policy, poverty, and social indicators.

3. **UNCTAD World Investment Report 2024** — Egypt chapter covers FDI trends and competitive positioning.

4. **CBE Annual Report FY2023-24** — Central Bank's own assessment including monetary policy rationale, reserves management, and banking sector health.

5. **Egypt Fiscal Monitor (IMF)** — Tracks public debt trajectory, subsidy reform progress, and social spending efficiency.

6. **Grand Egyptian Museum Opening Report** — Tourism Ministry assessment of GEM's projected economic impact.

</details>
